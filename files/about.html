About the association

Four decades of Crestfielders, one family.

Founded in 1978 by the pioneer sets of Crestfield College, our association exists to keep old friendships alive and to repay the school that shaped us.

Our mission

To unite all old students of Crestfield College, promote their welfare and advancement, and support the school through scholarships, infrastructure and mentorship.

Our vision

A globally connected alumni network whose giving-back guarantees that every promising Crestfielder — regardless of means — completes a first-class education.

Our story

Milestones

  1. 1978

    The association is formed

    The pioneer sets of ’74–’78 constitute the old students' body at the first homecoming.

  2. 1996

    First endowed scholarship

    A revolving fund is set up to keep bright students in school through hard times.

  3. 2014

    Chapters go global

    Lagos, Abuja and diaspora chapters formalise, linking members across three continents.

  4. 2024

    The Give-Back decade

    A ten-year plan to rebuild the library, science block and ICT lab is adopted.

Constitution & bylaws

Membership

Open to all who spent at least one full session at Crestfield College.

Governance

An elected Executive Council serves a two-year term; an AGM holds each December.

Chapters

Recognised branches of 15+ members may operate under the national constitution.

Funds

Dues and donations are ring-fenced for welfare and the Give-Back projects, audited yearly.
