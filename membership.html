Membership

Belong to something that outlasts a WhatsApp group.

Registered members get a verified alumni network, event access, welfare support and a real say in how our giving-back funds are spent.

Why join

Your membership, at work

🌍

Verified network

Search classmates by set, house and chapter in a members-only directory.

🎟️

Event access

Discounted homecoming passes and priority seating at set dinners.

💛

Welfare cover

Bereavement and hardship support coordinated by the Welfare Office.

🗳️

A vote

Elect the council and decide which projects the funds back next.

How to register

Three steps

  1. 1

    Tell us your set

    Your graduating year and house help us verify you and connect your classmates.

  2. 2

    Pay your dues

    Annual dues are ₦10,000 (₦150,000 for life membership). Every naira is audited.

  3. 3

    Get your card

    You'll receive a digital membership card and directory access within 48 hours.
